# Code of conduct

Chordleaf is a small project for people who write, arrange and rehearse songs. Everyone taking part in issues, discussions and pull requests should be able to do so without harassment, whatever their experience, background or musical taste.

## Expected behavior

- Be patient and kind, especially with people new to open source or to music notation.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people. Assume good intent and ask before assuming.
- Accept that the maintainer may decline a feature to keep Chordleaf focused.
- Share only invented or properly licensed song examples, and never other people's private data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, sexualized content, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and pressuring anyone after they have asked you to stop.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately through GitHub's [private reporting form](https://github.com/antoniomml/chordleaf/security/advisories/new) with "Conduct" in the title, or by opening an issue asking for a private contact without the details. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock conversations, or block participants who do not follow this code. It applies to all project spaces and to public spaces where someone represents the project.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
