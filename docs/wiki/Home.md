# Chordleaf help

Import a chord sheet, correct it and make your own version. Place chords on the right syllable, review the page layout and export PDF or print. Chordleaf works in English and Spanish; use **EN / ES** in the top bar. Your songs stay in your browser.

![Chordleaf song editor and page preview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/antoniomml/chordleaf/main/docs/images/workspace.png)
